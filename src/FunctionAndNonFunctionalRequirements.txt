Functional Requirements:

- User should be able to add item in the cart
- User should be able to remove Item or modify quantity of an item
- Capture the product price at the time it added to the cart
- Enforce a maximum quantity per product per cart
- Support three discount types - percentage off, flat amount off, buy-X-get-Y-free
- Allow only one discount per cart
- Track cart lifecycle through 3 states - Active, Checkout,
- Notify observer such as analytical team or marketing team when item is added to the cart
- Support one active cart per minute

Non-Functional Requirements:

- The system should handle concurrent cart modifications without data corruption
- The system should be modular and extensible to support future discount types
